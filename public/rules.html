Rules

Last updated October 25, 2026

WorldPixel only works if everyone grows their country the same way. These rules are part of the Terms of Service.

Play fair

  • One account per person. Don't make extra accounts to collect more free pixels.
  • No bots or scripts. Color pixels by hand. Automated clicking or calling the site's API from a program isn't allowed.
  • No exploiting bugs. If you find a way around the rules, report it instead of using it.
  • No selling. Don't buy, sell or trade accounts or pixels for money outside the game.

Keep it decent

  • No usernames with slurs, hate, sexual content, threats or personal information.
  • Don't pretend to be an admin or another player.
  • Don't use pixels to draw hateful symbols or messages.

Leave the site alone

  • Don't try to get into other people's accounts or into the site's servers.
  • Don't flood the site with requests or try to slow it down for others.

What happens if you break them

Admins can remove a username, undo pixels, or ban an account. A ban logs you out, keeps your username taken, and can put back every pixel the account colored. Bought pixels on a banned account aren't refunded. Admin decisions are final.

Report a problem

Found a bug or a player breaking the rules? Open an issue at github.com/SergeiZhdanov123/worldpixel/issues. For a security bug, describe it without posting how to exploit it.
